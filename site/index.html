About Lumaform

Lumaform is the motion studio of Nabeel Ahmad, a web developer in Pakistan. Each clip starts as a design idea and is built as a small program: the motion is written in React and rendered frame by frame with Remotion. Nothing is filmed or downloaded, and no AI video generator is used.

Every master is rendered in 4K UHD and checked before release for a clean loop point, smooth motion and steady brightness. Overlays come on pure black for Screen or Add blending, and the packs include a luma matte for keying.

Watch the 2026 showreel on YouTube.

Nabeel also builds fast WordPress websites. Need a loop in your brand colours? Get in touch.

  1. DesignA look, a palette and a motion idea, planned before any code.
  2. CodeThe animation is written as React components in Remotion, so every frame is calculated, not filmed.
  3. Render and checkRendered in 4K at 30 fps, then checked for the loop seam, smoothness and brightness.
